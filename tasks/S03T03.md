# S03T03 — Change detection (date A vs date B diff overlay)

**Difficulty:** hard
**Weight:** 0.3000 (share of project budget)
**Reward:** 0.003 SATS + 0.023 TON

Let users compare the same area across two dates and highlight what changed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Two date pickers: **date A** and **date B**.
- For each date, load imagery of the **same bbox** (filter by cloud cover — pick the least-cloudy pass near the selected date), reusing the Sentinel Hub / Copernicus source.
- Compute a difference between the two captures and **highlight the changed areas with an acid-green overlay** on top of the imagery.
- Provide a clear control to toggle the diff overlay on/off and to see A / B individually (e.g. swipe or toggle).
- Handle the no-data / too-cloudy / identical-image cases gracefully (clear message, no crash).
- Keep the current point and zoom; the diff recomputes for the current bbox.
- **Acceptance:** choose two dates with a visible real-world change → the changed region is highlighted; choosing two near-identical dates shows little/no highlight.
- Tests cover the date-selection + diff logic; CI stays green.
